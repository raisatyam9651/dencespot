## 2. What this SERP actually rewards

Five patterns hold across every competitor examined.

**1. Cost transparency is table stakes, not a differentiator.**
AK publishes ₹50,000–₹1,50,000. QHT publishes a Norwood Grade 2–7 table mapping grade → graft estimate → price, and markets per-graft transparency as a USP. Every clinic ranking for money terms names numbers. DenceSpot currently names none — every rate in the build is a `[CONFIRM]` placeholder. **This is the single largest competitive gap and it is not a technical problem; it needs a decision from the clinic.**

**2. Nobody wins with sector-level doorway pages.**
This is the most important finding, because it directly contradicts what the live site does. AK Clinics runs *city* pages (/delhi/, /gurgaon/, /bangalore/, /ludhiana/) and *technique* sub-pages — no sector pages. QHT runs city pages plus 12 Gurgaon *topic* sub-pages (FUE cost, crown, results, FAQs) — again no sector pages. Both name localities (Sohna Road, Cyber City, MG Road, DLF, Golf Course Road) **in prose on one page**, exactly the pattern already adopted in this build. The live DenceSpot site's ~172 locality pages are not just diluting signals in theory; no successful competitor is doing it in practice.

**3. Credentials are an arms race, and it is one to lose honestly.**
Competitors lead with ABHRS, FISHRS, ISHRS membership, named surgeon teams, and volume claims — QHT states 15,000+ transplants and "100% success rate"; the live DenceSpot site claims 98% success, 5k+ patients, 10+ years. The build correctly refuses to publish unevidenced claims. That is a short-term ranking disadvantage in a YMYL SERP and the right call anyway: a "100% success rate" claim is both clinically indefensible and exposed under NMC advertising norms. **Compete on specificity, not superlatives** — what is actually measured, photographed and reviewed.

**4. Review volume is the local pack.**
QHT 3,184+. Skinfinity 3,200+. DenceSpot ~109. Rating is not the problem — 4.8–4.9 is competitive. Volume and velocity are.

**5. FAQ depth is an open flank.**
AK runs 4 FAQs. QHT runs 7. The pages built here run 13–15 per page, written answer-first. That is a genuine advantage for both long-tail capture and AI-surface citation, and it costs nothing to keep extending.

| Where | Name | Address |

| **Practo** | Dencespot (Advanced & Best Hair Transplant Clinic, Skin Specialist, Dermatologist in Gurgaon India) | **Sector 38** |

Three different sectors (38, 39, 67A) and three different business names across the platforms Google uses to corroborate a listing. Two of the names are keyword-stuffed, which is a GBP guidelines violation in its own right and a suspension risk.

**This is the highest-severity finding in this document.** Citation consistency is a corroboration signal; contradiction across major aggregators actively suppresses local pack confidence. It is also the cheapest thing on this list to fix.

### Live-site problems this build already fixes
- `noindex` staging flag set correctly (`SITE_STAGING=true`)
- Duplicate/cannibalising URLs live: `/hair-transplant-in-gurgaon`, `/hair-transplant-2/`, `/best-hair-transplant/`
- "FUE/DHT Technology" — the DHT-is-a-hormone error, live and public. Corrected on the new DHI page.
- Unevidenced claims (98% success rate, 5k+ patients) — not carried into the build

## 4. Strategy — ranked by impact ÷ effort

### P0 — Do before launch. Blocks everything else.

**P0.1 — Collapse the NAP to one canonical version.**
Pick one name (`DenceSpot Clinic` — no keywords) and one address. Push it byte-identically to GBP, Practo, Justdial, magicpin, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect and Facebook. Correct the Practo (Sector 38), magicpin (Sector 67A) and Justdial ("D S Dencespot") entries individually. *Owner: clinic. Not a code task.*

**P0.2 — Decide and publish real prices.**
Per-graft rate, typical package ranges, PRP per-session and course prices. Competitors publish; the queries are overwhelmingly cost queries; the build has the slots waiting. *Owner: clinic. Unblocks `/hair-transplant-cost-in-gurgaon` and `/prp-hair-treatment-cost-in-gurgaon`.*

**P0.3 — Confirm GBP primary category.**
Primary category is the single strongest local-pack factor and a wrong one is the strongest negative. `Hair transplant clinic` vs `Dermatologist` vs `Skin care clinic` materially changes which queries the listing can surface for. Add up to four relevant secondary categories. *Owner: clinic.*

**P0.4 — Verify dencespot.com in Search Console.** Nothing in this document can be validated without it, and it is a five-minute job.

### P1 — Launch window

**P1.1 — Review engine.** From ~109 toward 300+ in twelve months. A post-treatment ask at the review appointment, a WhatsApp follow-up with the direct Google review link, and QR at reception. Two hard rules: never pre-screen for satisfaction before asking (Google fake-engagement violation, and FTC-actionable), and respond to every review — but never confirm or deny that a reviewer is a patient, which is the medical-privacy trap in review responses.

**P1.2 — Publish a Norwood-grade cost table.** QHT's strongest asset, and the build already plans a graft calculator (#8) and cost page (#3). Grade → graft range → indicative cost, with the arithmetic shown. This is the highest-value single page in the whole architecture.

**P1.3 — Retire the 172 locality pages with 301s to the relevant service page.** No competitor is winning with them, and they are actively diluting the site. Map each to its nearest service page, not to the homepage.

**P1.4 — Claim Bing Places and Apple Business Connect.** Bing feeds ChatGPT and Copilot; Apple feeds Siri and Apple Maps. Both are free, both are one-time, and neither is claimed as far as can be determined.

### P2 — Post-launch, ongoing

- **P2.1** Complete the technique and comparison pages (#17–19, #41–47) — already tracked in `BUILD-PROGRESS.md`.
- **P2.2** Keep extending FAQ depth. Competitors sit at 4–7 questions; this build sits at 13–15. Widen the gap.
- **P2.3** Pursue local "best of" list placements and Chamber/BBB-equivalent memberships — the strongest AI-visibility citation signals.
- **P2.4** Build out `/patient-stories/*` (#6) — first-hand experience is the E-E-A-T dimension no competitor in this set does well.
- **P2.5** Re-audit NAP quarterly. It drifts.
