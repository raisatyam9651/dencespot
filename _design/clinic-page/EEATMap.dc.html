What each section of the clinic page has to prove

Hair restoration is a Your-Money-or-Your-Life topic, so every section on the page is carrying a specific signal rather than just filling space. Ten sections, four pillars, and an honest status against each.

Experience

First-hand evidence the clinic and its cases are real.

Carried by: S04 inside the clinic, S07 result photography.

Expertise

A named, qualified, traceable person behind the advice.

Carried by: S03 the doctor, S08 content governance.

Authoritativeness

Recognition from somewhere the clinic does not control.

Carried by: S07 Google reviews. The clinic's weakest pillar.

Trustworthiness

Verifiable facts, stated limits, and nothing overclaimed.

Carried by: S01, S02, S05, S06, S09, S10 — most of the page.
