Who treats you

The doctor you meet is the doctor who operates

In many clinics the consultation is taken by a counsellor and the surgery is performed by whoever is available that day, often technicians. That is not how DenceSpot works. Dr. Nyra carries out the assessment, draws the hairline, plans the graft numbers and performs the procedure herself.

Assessment

Scalp, donor density and hair-loss pattern examined by Dr. Nyra — before any technique or price is discussed.

Hairline design

Drawn on your own face and agreed with you before surgery, planned for how it will age — not just how it looks next year.

The procedure

Performed by Dr. Nyra, supported by the clinic's trained surgical team. Follow-up reviews are with her too.
